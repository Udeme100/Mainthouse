ABOUT US

Professional. Reliable. Trusted. Since 2019, Mainthouse Cleaners has delivered expert cleaning, laundry, and fumigation services across Nigeria. What began as a recycling initiative has grown into a full-service brand built on quality, precision, and customer satisfaction. We serve homes and businesses nationwide, cleaner spaces, guaranteed.
