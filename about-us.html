ABOUT US

Welcome to Mainthouse Cleaners, your trusted partner for professional cleaning services in Nigeria. We specialize in providing a range of services, including laundry, fumigation, and general cleaning, with a focus on eco-friendliness and sustainability. Using self-produced environmentally friendly cleaning products, we are committed to delivering exceptional results while protecting the environment.

Our Vision

At Mainthouse Cleaners, our vision is to lead the way as Nigeria's most reliable cleaning service provider. We aim to enhance the lives of our clients by offering high-quality, eco-friendly cleaning solutions that are both effective and sustainable. By using self-produced cleaning products, we ensure better results for our clients while promoting environmental responsibility. Together, we strive to create a cleaner, healthier, and greener future for everyone.
