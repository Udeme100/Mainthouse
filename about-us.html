ABOUT US

Welcome to Mainthouse Cleaners, your trusted partner for professional cleaning services across Nigeria. Founded in 2019, we bring over six years of industry experience in delivering top-tier cleaning solutions to homes and businesses. Mainthouse began as a recycling company and has since evolved into a full-service cleaning and laundry brand. Today, we specialize in laundry, fumigation, and general cleaning services, tailored to meet the diverse needs of our clients with precision and excellence. Our commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction sets us apart as a leading name in Nigeria’s cleaning industry.

Our Vision

At Mainthouse Cleaners, our vision is to lead the way as Nigeria's most reliable cleaning service provider. We aim to enhance the lives of our clients by offering high-quality, eco-friendly cleaning solutions that are both effective and sustainable. We ensure better results for our clients, and together, we strive to create a cleaner, healthier, and greener future for everyone.
